# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-23

First public release.

- Screen recording with a floating, draggable camera bubble (circle, rounded square, 16:9; four sizes)
- Microphone and optional system audio on separate tracks
- 1080p at 30 or 60 fps (H.264) and 4K at 60 fps (HEVC)
- Pause / resume with no gaps, restart, discard
- 3-2-1 countdown and click highlights
- Control panel, recording bar and countdown hidden from the video
- Review window with trim, MP4 export, copy and delete
- Global shortcuts ⌥⌘R and ⌥⌘P, plus a menu bar item
- Guided permissions onboarding
- Universal binary for Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 15+
